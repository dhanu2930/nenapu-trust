Mythological Drama

Ashwaparva (ಅಶ್ವಪರ್ವ)

Directed by: Puneeth Kartha / Puneeth A S  |  Total Shows: 15 Shows

Ashwaparva is a grand, high-impact mythological drama that delves into the conflicts of duty, morality, and destiny inspired by ancient Indian epics. Staged with spectacular period costumes, powerful dialogues, and classical choreography, the play captures the tension between individual choices and cosmic destiny.

Directed jointly by Puneeth Kartha and Puneeth A S, this production has been hailed for its creative staging, classical background score, and powerful acting. The play's 15 shows across Karnataka have attracted wide appreciation for bringing epic themes to life with modern stagecraft and aesthetic sensibilities.

Production Highlights

Visual Grandeur

Traditional costumes, jewelry, and weapons designed to transport the audience to the epic age.

Award-winning Music

Original live classical score utilizing traditional percussion and wind instruments.

Director Spotlight

Puneeth A S

Theatre Artist & Director

A professionally trained actor from Rangayana with over 8 years of active theatre presence. Puneeth has directed acclaimed productions including Ashwaparva and Maayaa Dweepa, helming more than 20 plays and 125+ stage performances. He also serves as Theatre Faculty at Shree Daksha Performing Arts School.

Music Director Spotlight

Hariprasad D N

Music Director & Composer

An award-winning music director, composer, and theatre artist active since 2016. Trained at Sanchari Theatre and Gangubai Hanagal University, Hariprasad has composed music for 10 dramas, performed in 150+ shows, and accompanied percussion for over 30 productions. Winner of the B.V. Karanth Best Theatre Music Award in 2024.
