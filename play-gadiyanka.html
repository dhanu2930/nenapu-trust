Gadiyanka Kudimudda

Folk Play · Directed by Dilip B M · 10 Shows

Folk Play

Gadiyanka Kudimudda (ಗಡಿಯಂಕ ಕುಡಿಮುದ್ದ)

Directed by: Dilip B M  |  Total Shows: 10 Shows

Gadiyanka Kudimudda is a vibrant, music-filled folk play that celebrates the rich storytelling traditions and dialects of rural Karnataka. Rooted in traditional folk performance modes, the play features live instrumentation, energetic dances, and expressive storytelling that captures the rustic charm of rural life.

Directed by Dilip B M, this production has been highly praised for its authenticity, utilization of folk songs, and high-energy performances. Staged 10 times at various cultural festivals, it remains a key production highlighting Nenapu's mission of preserving and promoting folk arts.

Production Highlights

Folk Heritage

Preserving traditional Kannada folk tunes, oral stories, and dance styles on stage.

Live Instruments

Musical elements driven entirely by live folk instruments like Dollu, Kamsale, and Harmonium.

Director Profile

Dilip B M

Folk Theatre specialist

Dilip B M is an active folk artist and play director in Karnataka. His work focuses on reviving traditional folk forms and bringing village legends to life with modern production values, engaging rural and urban youth in the theatre movement.
