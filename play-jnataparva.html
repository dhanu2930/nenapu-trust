Jnataparva

Mythological Drama · Directed by Puneeth A S · 10 Shows

Mythological Drama

Jnataparva (ಜ್ಣಾತಪರ್ವ)

Script by: Karthik Bhat  |  Directed by: Puneeth A S  |  Total Shows: 10 Shows

Jnataparva is a mythological drama exploring themes of destiny, duty, and the cosmic order. The production blends traditional folk art forms with modern stagecraft to bring ancient narratives to contemporary audiences.

Production Highlights

Visual Grandeur

Traditional costumes, jewelry, and set pieces designed to evoke the epic era.

Music & Soundscape

Original score featuring classical percussion and vocal motifs.

Director Spotlight

Puneeth A S

Theatre Artist & Director

A professionally trained actor from Rangayana with over 8 years of active theatre presence. Puneeth has directed acclaimed productions including Ashwaparva and Maayaa Dweepa, helming more than 20 plays and 125+ stage performances. He also serves as Theatre Faculty at Shree Daksha Performing Arts School.

Playwright Spotlight

Karthik Bhat

Playwright & Script Writer

The creative mind behind the script of Jnataparva (ಜ್ಞಾತಪರ್ವ). Karthik Bhat weaves deep philosophical themes, intricate character dynamics, and rich dialogue into ancient mythological motifs, crafting a powerful narrative for the contemporary Kannada stage.

Music Director Spotlight

Hariprasad D N

Music Director & Composer

An award-winning music director, composer, and theatre artist active since 2016. Trained at Sanchari Theatre and Gangubai Hanagal University, Hariprasad has composed music for 10 dramas, performed in 150+ shows, and accompanied percussion for over 30 productions. Winner of the B.V. Karanth Best Theatre Music Award in 2024.
