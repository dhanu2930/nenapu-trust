Maayaa Dweepa

Tempest Adaptation · Directed by Puneeth Kartha / Puneeth A S · 12 Shows

Theatrical Adaptation

Maayaa Dweepa (ಮಾಯಾದ್ವೀಪ)

Written by: Shakespeare (Adapted)  |  Directed by: Puneeth Kartha / Puneeth A S  |  Total Shows: 12 Shows

Maayaa Dweepa represents a bold theatrical experiment by Nenapu, adapting William Shakespeare's late romance, The Tempest, for the Kannada stage. The adaptation blends classic Western dramatic themes of forgiveness, power, and illusion with traditional Indian folk aesthetics, local music, and magical realism.

Set on a mystical island simulated through creative stage props, lighting effects, and movement, the play chronicles Prospero's quest for justice and reconciliation. Staged 12 times in prominent theatre festivals, the play has won acclaim for its lyrical translation and the ethereal performances of its cast.

Production Highlights

Magical Realism

Stunning visual effects, mask work, and creative lighting to depict magic, spirits, and storms.

Lyrical Adaptation

Translated into rich Kannada verse that preserves the poetic depth of Shakespeare's work.

Director Spotlight

Puneeth A S

Theatre Artist & Director

A professionally trained actor from Rangayana with over 8 years of active theatre presence. Puneeth has directed acclaimed productions including Ashwaparva and Maayaa Dweepa, helming more than 20 plays and 125+ stage performances. He also serves as Theatre Faculty at Shree Daksha Performing Arts School.

Music Director Spotlight

Hariprasad D N

Music Director & Composer

An award-winning music director, composer, and theatre artist active since 2016. Trained at Sanchari Theatre and Gangubai Hanagal University, Hariprasad has composed music for 10 dramas, performed in 150+ shows, and accompanied percussion for over 30 productions. Winner of the B.V. Karanth Best Theatre Music Award in 2024.
