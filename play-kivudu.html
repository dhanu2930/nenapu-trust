Stage Comedy

Kivudu Saar Kivudu (ಕಿವುಡ ಸಾರ್ ಕಿವುಡ)

Written & Directed by: Shri Harsha M N  |  Total Shows: 60+

A side-splitting social satire, Kivudu Saar Kivudu is one of Nenapu's most celebrated and frequently performed plays. The narrative revolves around hilarious misunderstandings, miscommunications, and the comedy of errors that ensue when characters fail to truly listen to one another.

First staged shortly after the trust's inception, this comedy has toured widely across districts of Karnataka, performing to packed halls and rural crowds alike. The production stands as a testament to the trust's commitment to clean, wholesome family entertainment that also prompts reflection on contemporary societal behaviors.

Director Profile

Shri Harsha M N

Veteran Playwright & Director

Shri Harsha M N is an acclaimed director and dramatist associated with Nenapu Trust. Known for his keen eye for situational humor, Harsha has directed various landmark social dramas, fostering talent and staging comedies that resonate with diverse audiences.
