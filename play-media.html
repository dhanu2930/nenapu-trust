Media & Male Nage Hole

Tragedy & Comedy Double Bill · Directed by Uday Sosle / Shri Harsha M N · 10 Shows Each

Double Feature

Media & Male Nage Hole (ಮೀಡಿಯ ಮತ್ತು ಮಳೆ ನಗೆ ಹೊಳೆ)

Directors: Uday Sosle / Shri Harsha M N  |  Total Shows: 10 Shows Each

Nenapu's double feature presentation is a powerful showcase of theatrical contrast, staging the intense Greek classic tragedy Media side-by-side with the lighthearted social comedy Male Nage Hole. This double-bill tests the versatility of the trust's actors, who shift seamlessly from tragic, blood-stained mythology to cheerful contemporary satire.

Media, directed by Uday Sosle, focuses on the psychological depth and tragic desperation of Euripides' legendary heroine, rendered with minimalist design and haunting red-lit sequences. In stark contrast, Male Nage Hole, directed by Shri Harsha M N, features fast-paced dialogues, local dialects, and side-splitting physical comedy.

Production Highlights

Contrast of Emotions

An evening of theatre presenting the peak of human grief followed immediately by cheerful satire.

Acoustic Excellence

Live instrumental background tracking that shifts from operatic tragedy to upbeat folk-pop comedy tunes.

Director Details

Uday Sosle & Shri Harsha M N

Collaborative Directors

Uday Sosle specializes in adapting classical tragedies, focusing on physical expression and deep text-work. Shri Harsha M N is known for his social comedies and fast-paced staging. Together, they bring this double feature to life, pushing the boundaries of local stage productions.
