Featured Production

Spotlight on our current theatrical presentation on the Kannada stage.

New Presentation

Jnataparva (ಜ್ಞಾತಪರ್ವ)

Script by: Karthik Bhat  |  Duration: 1 Hour 15 Minutes

Our latest theatrical endeavor, Jnataparva, represents a deep exploration of human relationships and social dynamics. Developed under the artistic guidance of the Nenapu Trust, this presentation brings together a large team of dedicated artists and technicians to create a memorable experience on the Kannada stage.

25 On-Stage Actors
5 Backstage Crew
6 Music Crew
3 Technicians

Director Spotlight

Puneeth A S

Theatre Artist & Director

A professionally trained actor from Rangayana with over 8 years of active theatre presence. Puneeth has directed acclaimed productions including Ashwaparva and Maayaa Dweepa, helming more than 20 plays and 125+ stage performances. He also serves as Theatre Faculty at Shree Daksha Performing Arts School.

Playwright Spotlight

Karthik Bhat

Playwright & Script Writer

The creative mind behind the script of Jnataparva (ಜ್ಞಾತಪರ್ವ). Karthik Bhat weaves deep philosophical themes, intricate character dynamics, and rich dialogue into ancient mythological motifs, crafting a powerful narrative for the contemporary Kannada stage.

Music Director Spotlight

Hariprasad D N

Composer, Lyricist & Theatre Artist

An award-winning music director, composer, and theatre artist active since 2016. Trained at Sanchari Theatre and Gangubai Hanagal University, Hariprasad has composed music for 10 dramas, performed in 150+ shows, and accompanied percussion for over 30 productions. He was awarded the prestigious B.V. Karanth Best Theatre Music Award in 2024, the Best Theatre Music Award at Rangashree (2018), and back-to-back state-level accolades from Rangabhoomi Udupi (2024 & 2025).
